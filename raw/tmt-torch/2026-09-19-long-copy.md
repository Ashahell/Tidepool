# Long copy training (150k steps) — time doesn't unlock recall

> Source: tmt-torch run runs/copy150k (frac 1.0, dim128) + payload-CE + probe eval (inline output)
> Collected: 2026-09-19
> Published: 2026-09-19

## Measured 2026-09-19

150000 pure-copy steps (frac 1.0, dim128/layers4/lr 1e-4, RTRL): held-out
evals flat ~7.0 throughout (150k bpb=7.000); payload CE 6.494 (vs 7.923
at 30k, 7.229 at 60k — grinding down, still above uniform 5.545);
ingesting probe 0.0 at copy4 x after8/32.

Reading: recall does not emerge with time at this scale — 5x the steps
buys 1.4 CE points and zero exact recall. Time alone is ruled out.
Remaining: protocol redesign (cued recall, shorter gaps first), much
bigger scale, or structural memory.
